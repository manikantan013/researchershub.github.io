About Us

Protonical Researchers Hub is a Bangalore-based research, embedded systems, and product development organization dedicated to transforming innovative ideas into working prototypes, validated systems, and market-ready technologies. We support students, researchers, startups, MSMEs, and industries through complete technical and research execution across IoT, artificial intelligence, electronics, and applied engineering.

We bridge the gap between academic research and industrial deployment. From concept design and hardware development to PCB manufacturing, rapid prototyping, system testing, and documentation, we provide complete idea-to-product engineering solutions.

Our core technical expertise includes:

  • Embedded Systems and IoT Development
  • AI and Machine Learning Integration
  • Product Design and Hardware Prototyping
  • PCB Design and Manufacturing
  • 3D Design and Rapid Prototyping
  • Industrial Electronics and Smart Devices
  • Research and Development Consulting
  • Manufacturing and Investment Guidance

Protonical Researchers Hub actively collaborates with engineering colleges, universities, research scholars, MSMEs, and technology innovators across B.Tech, M.Tech, PhD, BCA, MCA, MSc, MBA, Food Technology, Nursing, Commerce, and Life Sciences to deliver technically sound and fundable solutions.

We also provide complete grant-based project development and proposal support for MSME, VGST, DST, BIRAC, SERB, and other government funding programs, enabling innovators to move from sanctioned funding to successful project execution.

Through industry-oriented internships, workshops, and faculty development programs, we provide hands-on exposure to real-world engineering, R&D, and product development workflows.

Led by experienced engineers and Ex-IISc professionals, Protonical Researchers Hub operates with a strong focus on engineering precision, research integrity, and practical implementation.

At Protonical Researchers Hub, we do not just support research — we engineer it into reality.
