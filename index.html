About Us

Protonical Researchers Hub is a Bengaluru (Bangalore)-based research, embedded systems and product development organization dedicated to transforming innovative ideas into working prototypes, validated systems and market-ready technologies. We support students, researchers, startups, MSMEs and industries through technical and research execution across IoT, artificial intelligence, electronics and applied engineering.

We bridge the gap between academic research and industrial deployment. From concept design, hardware and software development to PCB design, CAD modelling, 3D printing, prototype testing and technical documentation, we support projects from early concept to functional demonstration.

Our programs also cover final-year engineering project guidance, internships, industrial training, programming and hardware-development courses, school STEM and robotics workshops, faculty development programs, engineering training kits, MSME project mentorship and industry–academia collaboration. We are based in Bengaluru, India, and consider international enquiries according to project requirements and delivery arrangements.

Our core technical expertise includes:

  • Embedded Systems and IoT Development
  • AI and Machine Learning Integration
  • Product Design and Hardware Prototyping
  • PCB Design and Manufacturing
  • 3D Design and Rapid Prototyping
  • Industrial Electronics and Smart Devices
  • Research and Development Consulting
  • Manufacturing and Product Guidance

Protonical Researchers Hub works with engineering colleges, universities, research scholars, schools, MSMEs, startups and technology innovators. Project needs may span B.Tech, M.Tech, PhD, BCA, MCA, MSc, MBA, Food Technology, Nursing, Commerce and Life Sciences, depending on the technical scope and available expertise.

For MSME and grant-related projects, we can discuss technical feasibility, project planning, prototype development and documentation support. Funding eligibility, proposal requirements and program rules must be confirmed with the relevant funding body; we do not guarantee funding approval.

Through industry-oriented internships, workshops and faculty development programs, we provide hands-on exposure to real-world engineering, R&D and product development workflows.

Led by experienced engineers and Ex-IISc professionals, Protonical Researchers Hub operates with a focus on engineering precision, research integrity and practical implementation.

At Protonical Researchers Hub, we do not just support research — we engineer it into reality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about internships, student projects, engineering services, training and research collaboration.

Can Protonical Researchers Hub help with final-year engineering projects?
We can discuss project scoping, feasibility, embedded systems, IoT, PCB design, software/hardware integration, prototype development and technical documentation. The exact level of mentorship and implementation support is agreed with the student or institution and must follow applicable academic rules.
Do you provide internships and industrial training?
We accept internship enquiries for areas such as embedded systems, IoT, AI/ML, robotics, electronics, software and product development. Availability, duration, mode, project scope and any training-kit charges are confirmed before enrollment.
Can you provide CAD design, 3D printing or 3D printer servicing?
We can discuss CAD modelling, mechanical design, enclosures and 3D-printed prototypes. For printer servicing, troubleshooting or maintenance, please share the printer model, fault symptoms and location so we can confirm whether the requested service is supported.
Do you conduct school STEM, coding and robotics workshops?
Schools can enquire about practical activities covering coding, electronics, sensors, robotics and engineering concepts. Workshop design depends on students' age group, learning objectives, participant count, equipment and available time.
Can you help MSMEs with R&D projects and government support schemes?
We can discuss technical feasibility, prototype development, engineering execution and related documentation. Eligibility, procurement rules, submission requirements and funding decisions depend on the specific program and must be verified with the relevant authority. Funding or grant approval is not guaranteed.
Do you offer faculty development programs and industry–academia collaboration?
Colleges and institutions can contact us about technical workshops, faculty development, student projects, collaborative R&D and prototype engineering. The scope, schedule, responsibilities and commercial terms are agreed with each institution.
Can international clients enquire about projects?
Yes. We consider international enquiries and can discuss remote collaboration where suitable. Feasibility, shipping, on-site requirements, time zones, project scope and delivery arrangements are assessed individually.
