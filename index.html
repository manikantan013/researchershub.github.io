About Protonical Researchers Hub

Engineering research, practical development and product prototyping from Bengaluru, India.

Protonical Researchers Hub is a Bengaluru-based research, embedded systems and product development organization focused on turning concepts into functional prototypes and practical engineering solutions. We work with students, researchers, startups, MSMEs, educational institutions and industries.

Our work can cover concept development, hardware and software implementation, PCB design, CAD modelling, 3D printing, prototype testing, technical documentation and product engineering. Scope, deliverables, timelines and responsibilities are agreed for each engagement.

We also support enquiries related to final-year engineering projects, internships, industrial training, programming and hardware courses, school STEM workshops, robotics activities, faculty development, engineering training kits and industry–academia collaboration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about projects, training, prototyping and collaboration.

Can you help with final-year engineering projects?
We can discuss project scoping, feasibility, embedded systems, IoT, PCB design, software and hardware integration, prototype development and technical documentation. The agreed work must follow applicable institutional and academic rules.
Do you provide internships and industrial training?
Internship enquiries are welcome for embedded systems, IoT, AI/ML, robotics, electronics, software and product development. Availability, duration, mode, project scope and any training-kit charges are confirmed before enrollment.
Can you provide CAD design and 3D printing?
Contact us with the design or prototype requirements, dimensions, material preferences and intended application. We can review the requirements and confirm the suitable scope.
Do you conduct school STEM and robotics workshops?
Schools can enquire about activities covering coding, electronics, sensors, robotics and engineering concepts. Programs depend on the students' age group, learning objectives, equipment, participant count and available time.
Can you support MSME R&D or grant-related projects?
We can discuss feasibility, prototype development, technical execution and documentation. Eligibility, procurement rules and funding decisions depend on the specific program. Funding or grant approval is not guaranteed.
Can international clients contact Protonical Researchers Hub?
Yes. International enquiries are welcome. Remote collaboration, delivery arrangements, shipping, timelines and project scope are assessed individually.
