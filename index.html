About Us

Protonical Researchers Hub is a Bangalore-based research, embedded systems, and product development organization dedicated to transforming innovative ideas into working prototypes, validated systems, and market-ready technologies. We support students, researchers, startups, MSMEs, and industries through complete technical and research execution across IoT, artificial intelligence, electronics, and applied engineering.

We bridge the gap between academic research and industrial deployment. From concept design and hardware development to PCB manufacturing, rapid prototyping, system testing, and documentation, we provide complete idea-to-product engineering solutions.

Our core technical expertise includes:

  • Embedded Systems and IoT Development
  • AI and Machine Learning Integration
  • Product Design and Hardware Prototyping
  • PCB Design and Manufacturing
  • 3D Design and Rapid Prototyping
  • Industrial Electronics and Smart Devices
  • Research and Development Consulting
  • Manufacturing and Investment Guidance

Protonical Researchers Hub actively collaborates with engineering colleges, universities, research scholars, MSMEs, and technology innovators across B.Tech, M.Tech, PhD, BCA, MCA, MSc, MBA, Food Technology, Nursing, Commerce, and Life Sciences to deliver technically sound and fundable solutions.

We also provide complete grant-based project development and proposal support for MSME, VGST, DST, BIRAC, SERB, and other government funding programs, enabling innovators to move from sanctioned funding to successful project execution.

Through industry-oriented internships, workshops, and faculty development programs, we provide hands-on exposure to real-world engineering, R&D, and product development workflows.

Led by experienced engineers and Ex-IISc professionals, Protonical Researchers Hub operates with a strong focus on engineering precision, research integrity, and practical implementation.

At Protonical Researchers Hub, we do not just support research — we engineer it into reality.

Why Protonical Researchers Hub?

Protonical Researchers Hub is a Bangalore-based Embedded Systems, IoT and R&D Lab that transforms innovative ideas into fully working, test-ready and deployable engineering solutions. We act as a complete MSME R&D and product development partner for startups, research scholars, universities, and industries.

Our in-house teams handle electronics design, embedded firmware, AI integration, PCB development, mechanical design, and system testing — all under one roof. Whether you are developing a new product, executing a funded research project, or preparing for commercialization, we deliver with engineering accuracy and documentation compliance.

  • 01 Embedded Systems & IoT Development

    We design and develop embedded systems, IoT devices and smart electronics using microcontrollers, sensors, wireless communication, and cloud platforms. Our systems power industrial automation, medical devices, smart agriculture, and connected consumer products.

  • 02 Product Design, PCB & Hardware Prototyping

    We provide complete product engineering services including schematic design, PCB layout, firmware development, enclosure design, and functional hardware prototyping. We convert research concepts into manufacturable and test-ready products.

  • 03 AI, Machine Learning & Smart Systems

    We integrate AI and Machine Learning with IoT and embedded platforms for predictive analytics, automation, medical diagnostics, and smart decision-making systems using real-world sensor data.

  • 04 MSME, DST, BIRAC & Government Funded R&D

    We act as the technical execution and documentation partner for MSME, DST, VGST, BIRAC and SERB funded projects. Our services include proposal preparation, system development, testing, reporting, and audit-ready technical documentation.

  • 05 Industrial Training & Research Internships

    We provide industry-oriented internships, faculty development programs, and research training in Embedded Systems, IoT, AI, Robotics, and Product Development — helping students and professionals gain real-world engineering experience.
