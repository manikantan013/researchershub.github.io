Product Engineering Capabilities

Product Design and 3D Prototyping Services

Product development combines functional requirements, mechanical design, component compatibility and physical testing. We support the engineering stages needed to develop and evaluate a product concept, with the scope agreed according to your application.

Concept and Product Development

Convert an initial concept, sketch or requirement into a product architecture with defined dimensions, components, functional elements and design constraints.

CAD Modelling and Mechanical Design

Develop three-dimensional models, mechanical components, assemblies and design geometry using requirements, dimensions and reference information supplied for the project.

3D Printing and Rapid Prototyping

Prepare suitable models for additive manufacturing and evaluate printed parts for shape, fit, assembly, ergonomics and application-specific functional needs.

Enclosure and Housing Design

Design housings for electronics and mechanical assemblies, considering component placement, mounting features, access openings, serviceability and intended use.

Mechanical and Electronics Integration

Coordinate enclosure geometry with boards, sensors, cables, connectors, batteries and other components to support a workable product assembly.

Prototype Evaluation and Iteration

Inspect prototype fit, component clearances, assembly sequence and usability, then refine the design based on the observed results and agreed requirements.

Our Development Process

Six Steps from Product Idea to Physical Prototype

Our workflow helps connect product requirements with design, physical fabrication and iterative validation. The exact stages and deliverables depend on the complexity and requirements of each project.

01

Requirements and Concept Definition

Understand the intended application, user needs, size constraints, environment, components and functional expectations before beginning detailed design.

  • Requirements and use cases
  • Dimensions and physical constraints
  • Concept and design objectives
02

CAD Modelling and Design Development

Build a three-dimensional representation of the product, define component geometry and prepare assemblies that reflect the agreed functional requirements.

  • 3D parts and assembly models
  • Dimensions and mounting geometry
  • Design review and refinement
03

Material and Fabrication Planning

Choose appropriate materials and a practical fabrication method according to geometry, expected loading, appearance, cost constraints and intended prototype use.

  • Material suitability assessment
  • 3D printing orientation and preparation
  • Fabrication and finishing considerations
04

Prototype Fabrication

Prepare the design for the selected manufacturing process and produce or coordinate fabrication of physical parts according to the agreed scope.

  • Model preparation and file checks
  • Part fabrication or printing coordination
  • Initial inspection of produced parts
05

Assembly and Fit Evaluation

Assemble available parts and components, verify fit and accessibility, identify interference issues and assess the prototype against the intended use.

  • Mounting and fit verification
  • Clearance and interface checks
  • Usability and assembly observations
06

Design Iteration and Handover

Refine the design based on observed issues and agreed feedback, then deliver the specified models, fabrication files and technical documentation.

  • Design changes and corrections
  • Updated models and agreed file formats
  • Technical handover and documentation
Project Deliverables

What Can Be Included in a Product Design Project?

The deliverables below can be included where relevant to the agreed project. The final scope depends on the design inputs, intended use, fabrication method and handover requirements.

Deliverable Description
3D CAD model Three-dimensional product geometry or component assemblies in agreed native or exchange file formats.
Engineering drawings Relevant dimensions, views, interfaces and manufacturing information where required by the project.
3D-print-ready files Suitable manufacturing geometry exported in formats such as STL or 3MF when included in the design scope.
Physical prototype Fabricated or printed prototype parts, subject to the agreed material, size, quantity and production arrangement.
Assembly and fit observations Relevant findings from physical fit checks, component placement and prototype evaluation.
Revised design files Updated models and documentation reflecting the agreed design iterations and handover requirements.
