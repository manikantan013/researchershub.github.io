Engineering Capabilities

PCB Design and Hardware Prototyping Services

A successful electronic product begins with sound circuit design and a practical approach to hardware integration. We help translate functional requirements into circuit architectures and prototypes suitable for evaluation, testing and further development.

Electronic Circuit Design

Develop circuit concepts, select suitable electronic components, define interfaces and plan power, signal conditioning and control stages around the application.

Schematic and PCB Layout

Organize components and electrical connections into a documented schematic and PCB layout, considering board constraints, routing, clearances and intended fabrication.

Component Selection and BOM

Identify suitable components, check interface and rating requirements, and prepare a bill of materials aligned with the approved design and project constraints.

Embedded Hardware Integration

Integrate microcontrollers, sensors, communication modules, displays and other peripherals into a coordinated electronic system with appropriate electrical interfaces.

Prototype Assembly and Bring-Up

Support prototype assembly, initial power-up checks, interface verification, firmware integration and debugging according to the agreed project scope.

Design Review and Testing

Review design requirements, check connectivity and component compatibility, investigate prototype issues and document observations from functional testing.

Our Development Workflow

Six Steps from Design to Hardware Validation

We organize PCB and hardware projects into clear engineering stages, from requirements and circuit planning through layout, prototype integration and functional verification.

01

Requirements and Specifications

Define the device's purpose, electrical interfaces, power needs, operating environment, physical constraints and expected behaviour before design begins.

  • Functional and electrical requirements
  • Input/output and interface definition
  • Board dimensions and constraints
02

Circuit Architecture and Components

Plan functional blocks, select appropriate components and establish the electrical relationships between power, processing, sensing and communication circuits.

  • Component selection and rating checks
  • Power and signal path planning
  • Interface compatibility review
03

Schematic Capture and Design Review

Document the electronic circuit and review component connections, pin assignments, power nets and key electrical constraints before proceeding to the physical layout.

  • Schematic development
  • Connectivity and pin-assignment checks
  • Design rule and consistency review
04

PCB Layout and Fabrication Preparation

Arrange footprints and route connections to suit the design constraints. Prepare appropriate manufacturing outputs according to the agreed fabrication requirements.

  • Footprint placement and routing
  • Clearance and design rule checks
  • Manufacturing file preparation as scoped
05

Prototype Assembly and Bring-Up

Integrate the manufactured board with components and required firmware, then perform controlled initial power-up and interface verification.

  • Assembly and visual inspection
  • Power rail and connectivity checks
  • Firmware and peripheral integration
06

Functional Testing and Handover

Verify prototype behaviour against agreed requirements, troubleshoot identified issues and provide the approved technical outputs for the project.

  • Functional and interface testing
  • Issue investigation and design iteration
  • Documentation and agreed deliverables
Project Deliverables

What Can Be Included in a PCB Development Project?

Deliverables are defined according to the project scope, starting design inputs and agreed manufacturing or testing requirements. Not every project requires every item.

Deliverable Description
Circuit schematic Documented electronic connections, components and functional circuit blocks.
PCB layout files Board placement, routing and relevant design files, as agreed in the project scope.
Bill of materials Component references, part specifications, quantities and sourcing information where applicable.
Manufacturing outputs Fabrication or assembly files such as Gerbers, drill files and pick-and-place data when included in scope.
Prototype testing notes Functional checks, observed issues and test results collected during the agreed validation process.
Technical documentation Relevant connection details, integration notes and handover material as defined in the project agreement.
