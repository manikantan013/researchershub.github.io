Execution Framework

How we approach a funded project

The process is adapted to the project requirements, available funding period, expected technical outcomes and responsibilities of the participating organizations.

01

Review the Approved Scope

Review available objectives, project timelines, required outputs, technical constraints and applicable conditions before defining the support scope.

02

Define Activities & Milestones

Organize engineering tasks, dependencies, expected outputs and review points to establish a practical execution plan.

03

Plan Resources & Components

Identify relevant components, software, fabrication, tools and technical resources required for the agreed implementation.

04

Develop & Integrate

Carry out the agreed hardware, firmware, software, mechanical or experimental development tasks and integrate the resulting systems.

05

Review & Validate

Test agreed functionality, record results, investigate observed issues and review progress against the planned deliverables.

06

Document & Handover

Compile the agreed technical documentation, record the prototype status and hand over the specified project outputs.

Project Outputs

Clear technical deliverables and implementation records

Deliverables are agreed before work begins and depend on the technical objectives, approved project scope, contractual responsibilities and applicable funding conditions.

Where required, project owners should confirm reporting formats, approvals, procurement rules and any mandatory records directly against the relevant funding agreement or program guidance.

Important: Technical execution support does not automatically include grant application preparation, financial auditing, statutory certification, regulatory approval or submission to a funding authority. These activities must be agreed separately where applicable.
Discuss Scope & Deliverables
  • Technical Activity Plan Agreed tasks, requirements, milestones, expected outputs and development dependencies.
  • Design & System Architecture Records Applicable diagrams, interface details, component choices and implementation records.
  • Prototype or Integrated System The hardware, software or experimental prototype specifically included in the agreed scope.
  • Testing & Evaluation Records Test methods, recorded observations, results and known limitations where testing is included.
  • Technical Progress Documentation Relevant engineering progress records and milestone summaries within the agreed responsibility.
  • Project Handover Materials Applicable source files, BOMs, operating notes and final technical materials specified in the agreement.
